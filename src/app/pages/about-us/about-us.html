Who We Are

Eliya Foundation Trust is a non-profit organisation dedicated to serving the most vulnerable communities through humanitarian support, education, and sustainable development.

Since our founding, we have worked closely with local partners to provide food, healthcare, and safe shelter for families in need, while maintaining transparency and accountability at every step.
